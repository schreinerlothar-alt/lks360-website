Model guide

What the Siemens SGT-100 is used for

The SGT-100 is the smallest gas turbine in the Siemens Energy industrial range. It goes back to the Ruston Typhoon design and is built as a compact package for sites that need a few megawatts of power and heat.

  • Combined heat and power for factories, hospitals and district heating
  • Power for oil and gas platforms and production sites
  • Island operation where no grid is available

Siemens SGT-100 compared with neighbouring models

Compared with the SGT-300 and SGT-400 the SGT-100 is the choice for small loads. Buyers who need more output per unit usually move up to the SGT-300; buyers who compare across brands often look at the Solar Centaur 40 in the same small output class.

What to check when buying a used Siemens SGT-100

What is the Siemens SGT-100?

What was the SGT-100 called before?

The SGT-100 goes back to the Ruston Typhoon gas turbine. Siemens took over the range and renamed it SGT-100.

Is the SGT-100 suitable for combined heat and power?

Yes. The exhaust heat of the SGT-100 is commonly used in a heat recovery boiler for steam or hot water, which is a main reason why the model is used in industry and district heating.
