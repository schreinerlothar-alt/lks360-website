Model guide

What the GE Frame 9E is used for

The GE Frame 9E (MS9001E, for example PG9171E) is a heavy duty E class gas turbine for 50 Hz grids. It has a large installed fleet and is known for burning a wide range of fuels.

  • Utility simple cycle and combined cycle plants
  • Plants burning gas and liquid fuels
  • Industrial power for large sites

GE Frame 9E compared with neighbouring models

The 9E is the 50 Hz counterpart of the 60 Hz 7EA. In the 50 Hz E class it is often compared with the Siemens SGT5-2000E. The 9HA is the much larger H class model.

Price

Used GE Frame 9E price: what sets it

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC does not publish prices; each GE Frame 9E is priced by its owner and quoted on request with the stock number. The price depends on these points:

  • Version and rating: PG9171E or later 9E.03 and 9E.04 ratings, and any upgrade.
  • Hours, starts and the last overhaul: Equivalent operating hours, starts and the date and scope of the last hot gas path or major inspection matter more than the year of manufacture.
  • Overhaul records: A unit overhauled by the manufacturer or an authorised shop, with reports, sells faster than one without documents.
  • Condition: New and unused, overhauled, in operation or preserved: each condition is priced differently.
  • Scope of supply: Generator, controls, enclosure, fuel system, inlet and exhaust system, transformer and spare parts included or not.
  • Frequency and voltage: A 60 Hz unit and a 50 Hz unit serve different markets; the generator voltage has to fit the buyer's grid.
  • Emission system: DLE, water injection or diffusion combustion decides whether the unit meets the emission limits at the buyer's site.
  • Location and logistics: Dismantling, transport of heavy lifts and import duties are part of the buyer's total cost.

What is the GE Frame 9E?

The GE Frame 9E is an industrial heavy duty gas turbine by GE Vernova. Output: 132 MW (9E.03) to 147 MW (9E.04), simple cycle net. Typical applications: simple cycle, combined cycle, industrial CHP and mechanical drive. Source: GE Vernova, 9E product page.

What does PG9171E mean?

PG9171E is a GE model designation of the Frame 9E package for generator drive.

What is the difference between the Frame 9E and the 7EA?

The 9E is built for 50 Hz grids and the 7EA for 60 Hz grids. See Frame 9E vs 7EA for the manufacturer data.

What is the output of a GE Frame 9E?

GE Vernova states 132 MW (9E.03) to 147 MW (9E.04) in simple cycle, net, at 50 Hz. Older PG9171E units are rated lower; the units listed here are rated 126 MW and 243 MW for two units by their owners.
