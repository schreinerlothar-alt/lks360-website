Model guide

What the GE 7FA (7F) is used for

The GE 7FA is a heavy duty F class gas turbine for 60 Hz grids. It is one of the most installed F class turbines in combined cycle plants.

  • Utility combined cycle plants
  • Simple cycle peaking plants
  • Repowering of older plants

GE 7FA (7F) compared with neighbouring models

The 7FA is the larger, more efficient 60 Hz step above the E class 7EA. The H class 9HA is the 50 Hz flagship.
