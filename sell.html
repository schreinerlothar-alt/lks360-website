For equipment owners

Sell your surplus power equipment

Gas turbines, steam turbines, generator sets and complete plants. We present your unit to buyers with its technical data. Your name and site stay confidential.

How it works for owners

From listing to purchase contract

1

Unit data

You send model, year, output, hours, condition and your price idea.

2

Listing

The unit gets a stock number and is offered to buyers without your name or site.

3

Qualified buyer

Full data and inspection only after the buyer's letter of intent and with your consent.

4

Contract

You sign the purchase contract with the buyer. Payment goes from buyer to you. Our commission is agreed in writing.

Your email program opens with the message. Please press send there. If nothing opens, write to manager@lks360.com.

What we need

Data for a listing

  • Manufacturer, model and output
  • Year, operating hours since new and since last overhaul
  • Frequency, fuel and condition
  • Scope: generator, HRSG, balance of plant, spare parts
  • Available documents and photos

We confirm availability with you before every offer and remove sold units from our inventory.
