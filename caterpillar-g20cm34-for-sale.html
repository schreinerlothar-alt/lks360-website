Model guide

What the Caterpillar G20CM34 is used for

The Caterpillar G20CM34 is a medium speed gas engine with 20 cylinders from the MaK engine line of Caterpillar. It is used in gas fired power plants and as generator set for industrial power.

  • Gas engine power plants
  • Industrial base load power
  • Flexible power to support grids

Caterpillar G20CM34 compared with neighbouring models

Compared with high speed gas engines such as the Jenbacher J620, the G20CM34 is a larger medium speed engine with higher output per unit. The Wärtsilä 34SG is a comparable medium speed gas engine.
