Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Does LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC own the equipment?

No. We are an independent broker. The equipment belongs to its owner until the sale, and we do not take title to it.

Who signs the purchase contract?

The buyer and the owner sign the purchase contract directly. Payment terms are agreed between them in that contract.

How is LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC paid?

We work on commission. The commission is agreed in writing before the deal is closed and paid by bank transfer after the purchase contract is signed. Purchase payments go directly from buyer to owner and never pass through accounts of LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC.

When do I receive serial numbers, location and full technical data?

After receipt of a letter of intent. Before that we send an offer with model, output, frequency, condition and the owner's conditions.

Can I inspect the unit before buying?

Yes. After the letter of intent the buyer can inspect the unit on site. The inspection terms are agreed with the owner.

I own a turbine. Will you publish my name or site?

No. We present the unit with its technical data only. Your name and the site location stay confidential until a buyer has signed a letter of intent and you agree.
