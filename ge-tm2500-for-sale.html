Model guide

What the GE TM2500 is used for

The GE TM2500 is a trailer mounted gas turbine generator built around the LM2500 aeroderivative engine. It is designed to be moved by road and set up quickly where power is needed.

  • Emergency and temporary power
  • Grid support during plant outages
  • Fast track power for industrial projects

GE TM2500 compared with neighbouring models

The TM2500 uses the same engine family as the stationary LM2500 package but comes on trailers. Buyers choose it when mobility and short installation time matter more than a permanent foundation.

Questions and answers

GE TM2500: frequent questions

What is the GE TM2500?

The GE TM2500 is a mobile aeroderivative gas turbine by GE Vernova. Output: 34.5 MW net (DLE) and 34.6 MW net (SAC) at 50 Hz. Typical applications: bridge to permanent power, emergency and backup power, isolated locations. Source: GE Vernova, TM2500 product page.

What is the difference between the TM2500 and the LM2500?

The TM2500 is a mobile, trailer mounted package with an LM2500 family engine. The LM2500 is usually installed as a stationary package. See LM2500 vs TM2500 for the details.

How fast can a TM2500 be installed?

The TM2500 is designed for short installation times. The real time depends on site preparation, fuel supply and grid connection.
