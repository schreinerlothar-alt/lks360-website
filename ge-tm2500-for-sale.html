Model guide

What the GE TM2500 is used for

The GE TM2500 is a trailer mounted gas turbine generator built around the LM2500 aeroderivative engine. It is designed to be moved by road and set up quickly where power is needed.

  • Emergency and temporary power
  • Grid support during plant outages
  • Fast track power for industrial projects

GE TM2500 compared with neighbouring models

The TM2500 uses the same engine family as the stationary LM2500 package but comes on trailers. Buyers choose it when mobility and short installation time matter more than a permanent foundation.

Price

GE TM2500 price: what sets it

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC does not publish prices; each GE TM2500 is priced by its owner and quoted on request with the stock number. The price of a used TM2500 depends on these points:

  • Generation or version: Later versions usually reach higher output and efficiency; the version stated by the owner is checked against the engine data plate and documents.
  • Hours and cycles since the last overhaul: Engine hours, starts and the date and scope of the last overhaul matter more than the year of manufacture.
  • Overhaul records: An engine back from the manufacturer or an authorised shop with full records sells faster than one without documents.
  • Condition: New and unused, zero time since overhaul, in operation or preserved: each condition is priced differently.
  • Scope of supply: Generator, enclosure, controls, fuel system, step up transformer and spare parts included or not.
  • Trailers and mobile equipment: Number and condition of trailers, road permits and the mobile balance of plant decide how fast the unit can move to a new site.
  • Frequency and voltage: A 60 Hz unit for the Americas and a 50 Hz unit for Europe, Africa or Asia are separate markets; dual frequency units widen the buyer group.
  • Emission system: DLE or water injected combustion decides whether a unit meets the emission limits at the buyer's site.
  • Location and logistics: Dismantling, transport, road permits and import duties are part of the buyer's total cost.

Versions

GE TM2500 versions: TM2500, TM2500+ and Gen 4, 5, 6

The TM2500 has been built in several versions. The version of a used unit is taken from its documents, not from the listing title.

TM2500The first mobile package built around the LM2500 aeroderivative gas turbine; replaced by the TM2500+.
TM2500+Introduced by GE in 2008: packaged on a two-trailer system, up to 34 MW, with the LM2500 gas turbine and a Brush 170ER generator. GE stated a 30 percent increase in power from a footprint 75 percent smaller than its predecessor.
Gen 4, Gen 5, Gen 6Generation labels used by owners and traders for TM2500 units. They are not used on the current GE Vernova product page, so the generation stated by the owner is checked against the engine and generator documents.
TM2500 DLE and SACCurrent GE Vernova range: 34.5 MW net (DLE) and 34.6 MW net (SAC) at 50 Hz, ISO conditions.

Manufacturer data for the model type, not the data of a specific unit offered. Sources: GE press release, 2 December 2008 (TM2500+), GE Vernova, TM2500 product page.

What is the GE TM2500?

The GE TM2500 is a mobile aeroderivative gas turbine by GE Vernova. Output: 34.5 MW net (DLE) and 34.6 MW net (SAC) at 50 Hz. Typical applications: bridge to permanent power, emergency and backup power, isolated locations. Source: GE Vernova, TM2500 product page.

What is the difference between the TM2500 and the LM2500?

The TM2500 is a mobile, trailer mounted package with an LM2500 family engine. The LM2500 is usually installed as a stationary package. See LM2500 vs TM2500 for the details.

How fast can a TM2500 be installed?

The TM2500 is designed for short installation times. The real time depends on site preparation, fuel supply and grid connection.
