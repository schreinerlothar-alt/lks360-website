Model guide

What the GE LM6000 is used for

The GE LM6000 is a two shaft aeroderivative gas turbine developed from the CF6-80C2 aircraft engine. It is widely used for peaking, cogeneration and fast start power plants.

  • Peaking plants with frequent starts
  • Cogeneration and combined cycle
  • Power barges and fast track plants

GE LM6000 compared with neighbouring models

The LM6000 is larger than the LM2500 and the successor of the LM5000. Versions include PA, PC, PD and PF; SPRINT units use water spray intercooling for more output on hot days.

Price

Used GE LM6000 price: what sets it

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC does not publish prices; each GE LM6000 is priced by its owner and quoted on request with the stock number. The price of a used LM6000 depends on these points:

  • Generation or version: Later versions usually reach higher output and efficiency; the version stated by the owner is checked against the engine data plate and documents.
  • Hours and cycles since the last overhaul: Engine hours, starts and the date and scope of the last overhaul matter more than the year of manufacture.
  • Overhaul records: An engine back from the manufacturer or an authorised shop with full records sells faster than one without documents.
  • Condition: New and unused, zero time since overhaul, in operation or preserved: each condition is priced differently.
  • Scope of supply: Generator, enclosure, controls, fuel system, step up transformer and spare parts included or not.
  • Frequency and voltage: A 60 Hz unit for the Americas and a 50 Hz unit for Europe, Africa or Asia are separate markets; dual frequency units widen the buyer group.
  • Emission system: DLE or water injected combustion decides whether a unit meets the emission limits at the buyer's site.
  • Location and logistics: Dismantling, transport, road permits and import duties are part of the buyer's total cost.

What is the GE LM6000?

The GE LM6000 is an aeroderivative gas turbine by GE Vernova. Output: about 57 MW simple cycle (51.1 MW PC Sprint, 56.9 MW PF+ Sprint net). Typical applications: fast start and grid support, backup power, flexible generation and CHP. Source: GE Vernova, LM6000 product page.

What does SPRINT mean on an LM6000?

SPRINT stands for spray intercooling. Water is sprayed into the compressor to cool the air, which raises output, mainly on hot days.

What is the difference between LM6000 PA, PC and PD?

They are generations of the LM6000. PC and PD versions came later than the PA and differ in output and combustion system. The data sheet of each unit states the version.

What does a used GE LM6000 cost?

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC quotes the price of each listed LM6000 on request with the stock number. It depends on version, hours and cycles since overhaul, overhaul records, condition, scope, frequency, emission system and location.

Is the wiring harness included with a used LM6000?

Harnesses, sensors and control cabling belong to the engine and package scope. The scope list of each unit states what is included; LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC lists complete units and packages, not single spare parts.

What is the difference between LM6000 SAC and DLE?

SAC is the single annular combustor, which GE Vernova describes as water injected; DLE is dry low emissions combustion. GE Vernova offers the current LM6000 in both configurations, and the data sheet of a used unit states which one it has.
