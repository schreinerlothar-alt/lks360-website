Model guide

What the GE 9HA is used for

The GE 9HA is a heavy duty H class gas turbine for 50 Hz grids and one of the largest air cooled gas turbines on the market. It is built for large combined cycle power plants.

  • Large utility combined cycle plants
  • Base load power with high efficiency
  • New build plants that need a large block of power

GE 9HA compared with neighbouring models

The 9HA is far larger than the E class 9E. It is offered in the versions 9HA.01 and 9HA.02 with different outputs.

Price

Used GE 9HA price: what sets it

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC does not publish prices; each GE 9HA is priced by its owner and quoted on request with the stock number. The price depends on these points:

  • Version: 9HA.01 or 9HA.02, and whether the unit is new and unused or has run.
  • Hours, starts and the last overhaul: Equivalent operating hours, starts and the date and scope of the last hot gas path or major inspection matter more than the year of manufacture.
  • Overhaul records: A unit overhauled by the manufacturer or an authorised shop, with reports, sells faster than one without documents.
  • Condition: New and unused, overhauled, in operation or preserved: each condition is priced differently.
  • Scope of supply: Generator, controls, enclosure, fuel system, inlet and exhaust system, transformer and spare parts included or not.
  • Frequency and voltage: A 60 Hz unit and a 50 Hz unit serve different markets; the generator voltage has to fit the buyer's grid.
  • Emission system: DLE, water injection or diffusion combustion decides whether the unit meets the emission limits at the buyer's site.
  • Location and logistics: Dismantling, transport of heavy lifts and import duties are part of the buyer's total cost.

The GE 9HA is an industrial heavy duty gas turbine by GE Vernova. Output: 448 MW (9HA.01) and 571 MW (9HA.02), simple cycle net. Typical applications: combined cycle power plants. Source: GE Vernova, 9HA product page.
