Model guide

What the Siemens SGT5-2000E is used for

The SGT5-2000E is a Siemens Energy heavy duty gas turbine for 50 Hz grids. It goes back to the V94.2 and is one of the established E class turbines in utility power plants.

  • Utility combined cycle power plants
  • Simple cycle peaking and base load plants
  • Cogeneration for large industrial sites

Siemens SGT5-2000E compared with neighbouring models

The SGT5-2000E is the 50 Hz version; the 60 Hz counterpart is the SGT6-2000E. In the E class it is usually compared with the GE Frame 9E.
