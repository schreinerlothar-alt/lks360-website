Model guide

What the Siemens SST-300 is used for

The SST-300 is a Siemens Energy industrial steam turbine with a single casing for small and medium outputs. It is used in industrial cogeneration, biomass and waste heat plants.

  • Biomass and waste to energy plants
  • Industrial cogeneration
  • Waste heat recovery

Siemens SST-300 compared with neighbouring models

The SST-300 covers smaller outputs than the SST-400. It is offered as condensing or back pressure turbine and often with a gearbox between turbine and generator.

Price

Siemens SST-300 price: what sets it

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC does not publish prices; each Siemens SST-300 is priced by its owner and quoted on request with the stock number. The price depends on these points:

  • Condition: New and unused with preservation records, or used with operating hours: each is priced differently.
  • Steam conditions: How close the design inlet pressure, temperature, flow and exhaust pressure are to the buyer's plant.
  • Type: Condensing, back pressure or extraction turbine.
  • Scope of supply: Gearbox, generator, condenser, lube oil system and controls included or not.
  • Frequency and voltage: Generator frequency and voltage against the buyer's grid.
  • Documents: Data sheet, heat balance, test reports and preservation records.
  • Location and logistics: Dismantling, transport and import duties are part of the buyer's total cost.

Versions

Siemens SST-300 configurations

Siemens Energy states an output of up to 60 MW and a speed of up to 12,000 rpm for the SST-300. Each unit is built for given steam conditions:

CondensingExhausts into a condenser for maximum power output.
Back pressureExhausts steam at a pressure a process can still use, for cogeneration.
ExtractionTakes process steam out at one or more stages.
DriveOften with a gearbox between the turbine and the generator.
