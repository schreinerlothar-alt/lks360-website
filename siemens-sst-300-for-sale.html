Model guide

What the Siemens SST-300 is used for

The SST-300 is a Siemens Energy industrial steam turbine with a single casing for small and medium outputs. It is used in industrial cogeneration, biomass and waste heat plants.

  • Biomass and waste to energy plants
  • Industrial cogeneration
  • Waste heat recovery

Siemens SST-300 compared with neighbouring models

The SST-300 covers smaller outputs than the SST-400. It is offered as condensing or back pressure turbine and often with a gearbox between turbine and generator.
