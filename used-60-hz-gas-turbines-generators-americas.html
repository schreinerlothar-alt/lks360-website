Can a 50 Hz unit be used in the United States?

The US grid runs at 60 Hz. A 50 Hz unit usually needs a different gearbox or generator, so buyers in the Americas normally look for 60 Hz units or units specified for both frequencies. See the used gas turbine FAQ for details.
