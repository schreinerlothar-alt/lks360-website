Model guide

What the GE 7EA is used for

The GE 7EA is a heavy duty E class gas turbine for 60 Hz grids. It is a common choice for peaking and combined cycle plants in the Americas and other 60 Hz regions.

  • Peaking and mid merit power plants
  • Combined cycle and cogeneration
  • Dual fuel plants with liquid fuel backup

GE 7EA compared with neighbouring models

The 7EA is the 60 Hz E class model; the Frame 9E is its 50 Hz counterpart. For larger output on 60 Hz grids buyers look at the F class 7FA.
