Model guide

What the Solar Titan is used for

Titan is the largest gas turbine family of Solar Turbines, a Caterpillar company. The Titan 130 and Titan 250 are used for power generation and for driving compressors in oil and gas.

  • Gas compression on pipelines and production sites
  • Industrial power generation and cogeneration
  • Offshore power and mechanical drive

Solar Titan compared with neighbouring models

The Titan 250 is larger than the Titan 130. Both are larger than the Solar Centaur, Mars and Taurus families. Many Titan units are built for mechanical drive, so the drive type matters when a buyer needs a generator set.

What is the difference between the Titan 130 and the Titan 250?

Both belong to the Titan family of Solar Turbines. The Titan 250 has the higher output. The data sheet of each unit states the rating.

Can a mechanical drive Titan be used for power generation?

A mechanical drive package is built to drive a compressor. Conversion to generator drive needs a different package and is assessed case by case.
