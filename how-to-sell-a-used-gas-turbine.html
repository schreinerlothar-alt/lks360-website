How do I sell a used gas turbine?

A step by step guide for owners of used and surplus gas turbines.

Short answer: Collect the unit data, set your net price, offer the unit through one sales route at one price, release full data only to buyers who sign a letter of intent, sign the purchase contract directly with the buyer and receive payment through escrow or an irrevocable letter of credit. The same steps apply to steam turbines and generator sets.

Step by step

  1. Collect the unit data. Manufacturer and model, output, year, operating hours since new and since the last overhaul, frequency, fuel, condition, scope of supply (generator, HRSG, balance of plant, spare parts) and the documents and photos you have.
  2. Set your net price. Decide the amount you want to receive for the unit as it stands, and whether dismantling, loading and transport are included or are for the buyer.
  3. Choose one sales route. You can sell directly, through a dealer who buys the unit, or through a broker. If several brokers offer the same unit at different prices, buyers see it everywhere and the price drops. One exclusive mandate keeps one price and one data set in the market.
  4. Screen buyers. Give price and key data first. Release serial numbers, site, full documents and inspection only after a signed letter of intent and with your consent.
  5. Inspection and contract. The buyer inspects the unit on site under terms you agree. Buyer and owner sign the purchase contract directly.
  6. Payment and handover. The purchase price is paid through an escrow agent or an irrevocable letter of credit before the unit leaves the site.

Which data does a buyer need first?

Model, output, year, operating hours since new and since the last overhaul, frequency, fuel, condition, scope of supply and the region of the site. Serial numbers and the exact site follow after a letter of intent.

Should I give my turbine to several brokers?

It usually lowers the price. The same unit then appears at different prices with different data, and serious buyers start to doubt it. An exclusive mandate for a fixed period gives one offer, one price and one consistent data set.

Do I pay anything in advance to sell through LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC?

No. There is no upfront fee. Our income is agreed in writing and only arises when the unit is sold. Details: exclusive sales mandate

Will my company name and site be published?

No. The unit is presented with its technical data and a stock number only. Name and site stay confidential until a buyer has signed a letter of intent and you agree.

How is the purchase price paid to the owner?

Through escrow or an irrevocable letter of credit agreed in the purchase contract. Purchase payments never pass through accounts of LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC.

Can I sell a steam turbine or generator set the same way?

Yes. The steps are the same for steam turbines, generator sets and complete power plants. Send the unit data at exclusive sales mandate
