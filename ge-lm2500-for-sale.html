Model guide

What the GE LM2500 is used for

The GE LM2500 is an aeroderivative gas turbine developed from the CF6 aircraft engine. It is used in industrial power generation, on ships and in oil and gas.

  • Power generation and cogeneration
  • Mechanical drive for compressors
  • Marine propulsion and offshore power

GE LM2500 compared with neighbouring models

The LM2500 family includes the base LM2500, the LM2500+ and the LM2500+G4 with higher output. The TM2500 is a trailer mounted package built around the LM2500. The LM6000 is the larger GE aeroderivative.

Price

Used GE LM2500 price: what sets it

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC does not publish prices; each GE LM2500 is priced by its owner and quoted on request with the stock number. The price depends on these points:

  • Version: Base LM2500, LM2500+ or LM2500+G4: later versions have more output, so the version decides the comparison.
  • Hours, starts and the last overhaul: Equivalent operating hours, starts and the date and scope of the last hot gas path or major inspection matter more than the year of manufacture.
  • Overhaul records: A unit overhauled by the manufacturer or an authorised shop, with reports, sells faster than one without documents.
  • Condition: New and unused, overhauled, in operation or preserved: each condition is priced differently.
  • Scope of supply: Generator, controls, enclosure, fuel system, inlet and exhaust system, transformer and spare parts included or not.
  • Frequency and voltage: A 60 Hz unit and a 50 Hz unit serve different markets; the generator voltage has to fit the buyer's grid.
  • Emission system: DLE, water injection or diffusion combustion decides whether the unit meets the emission limits at the buyer's site.
  • Location and logistics: Dismantling, transport of heavy lifts and import duties are part of the buyer's total cost.

What is the GE LM2500?

The GE LM2500 is an aeroderivative gas turbine by GE Vernova. Output: about 37 MW simple cycle (LM2500XPRESS+ G4 34.5 MW, G5 36.3 MW net, 50 Hz). Typical applications: fast power and cycling, grid support, data centers, reserve power, CHP, industrial. Source: GE Vernova, LM2500 product page.

What is the difference between the LM2500, LM2500+ and LM2500+G4?

They are versions of the same aeroderivative family. The LM2500+ and LM2500+G4 have higher output than the base LM2500. The data sheet of each unit states the exact version.

Can an LM2500 engine be exchanged?

Aeroderivative engines can be removed and exchanged as a whole, which shortens outages. Engine exchange and lease options depend on the depot and are clarified case by case.

What is the output of a GE LM2500?

GE Vernova states about 37 MW in simple cycle for the current LM2500, with 34.5 MW (G4) and 36.3 MW (G5) net at 50 Hz for the LM2500XPRESS+ package. Older units have less; the data sheet of each unit states its rating.
