For buyers

What to send us

Model or output class, frequency (50 or 60 Hz), fuel (gas, liquid or dual fuel), delivery location, timeline, and whether you need a single unit or a complete plant with HRSG and balance of plant.

We match your requirement with units confirmed by their owners and send an offer with the owner's conditions. Full technical data, serial numbers and the unit location follow after receipt of a letter of intent.
