GE LM2500 vs GE TM2500

Manufacturer data side by side, with the used units of both models listed on lks360.com.

Short answer: The GE LM2500 is an aeroderivative gas turbine by GE Vernova with an output of about 37 MW simple cycle (LM2500XPRESS+ G4 34.5 MW, G5 36.3 MW net, 50 Hz). The GE TM2500 is a mobile aeroderivative gas turbine by GE Vernova with an output of 34.5 MW net (DLE) and 34.6 MW net (SAC) at 50 Hz. Frequency: GE LM2500 50 Hz and 60 Hz options, GE TM2500 50 Hz (ratings shown). Sources: GE Vernova, LM2500 product page; GE Vernova, TM2500 product page.
