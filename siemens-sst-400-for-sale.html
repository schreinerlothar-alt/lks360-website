Model guide

What the Siemens SST-400 is used for

The SST-400 is a Siemens Energy industrial steam turbine with a single casing. It is used as condensing or back pressure turbine, with or without steam extraction.

  • Industrial power plants and cogeneration
  • Waste to energy and biomass plants
  • Steam turbine in combined cycle blocks

Siemens SST-400 compared with neighbouring models

The SST-400 is the larger of the two Siemens industrial steam turbines listed here; the SST-300 covers smaller outputs. Whether condensing or back pressure fits depends on whether the plant needs process steam.
