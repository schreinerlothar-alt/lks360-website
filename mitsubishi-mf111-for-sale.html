Model guide

What the Mitsubishi MF111 is used for

The MF111 is an industrial gas turbine from Mitsubishi Heavy Industries in the small to medium output class. It is mostly used for cogeneration with a heat recovery steam generator.

  • Industrial cogeneration with process steam
  • Combined cycle for industrial sites
  • Power for factories in Asia
