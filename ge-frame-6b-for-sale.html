Model guide

What the GE Frame 6B is used for

The GE Frame 6B (MS6001B) is a heavy duty gas turbine known for reliability and fuel flexibility. It is common in industrial cogeneration and in refineries.

  • Industrial cogeneration and process steam
  • Refinery and petrochemical power
  • Combined cycle plants in the lower utility range

GE Frame 6B compared with neighbouring models

The Frame 6B sits between the Frame 5 and the 7EA and 9E. Its fuel flexibility, including liquid fuels, makes it a frequent choice for industrial sites.

Price

Used GE Frame 6B price: what sets it

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC does not publish prices; each GE Frame 6B is priced by its owner and quoted on request with the stock number. The price depends on these points:

  • Version and rating: The rating on the data plate and any uprate decide the output of a used Frame 6B.
  • Hours, starts and the last overhaul: Equivalent operating hours, starts and the date and scope of the last hot gas path or major inspection matter more than the year of manufacture.
  • Overhaul records: A unit overhauled by the manufacturer or an authorised shop, with reports, sells faster than one without documents.
  • Condition: New and unused, overhauled, in operation or preserved: each condition is priced differently.
  • Scope of supply: Generator, controls, enclosure, fuel system, inlet and exhaust system, transformer and spare parts included or not.
  • Frequency and voltage: A 60 Hz unit and a 50 Hz unit serve different markets; the generator voltage has to fit the buyer's grid.
  • Emission system: DLE, water injection or diffusion combustion decides whether the unit meets the emission limits at the buyer's site.
  • Location and logistics: Dismantling, transport of heavy lifts and import duties are part of the buyer's total cost.

What is the GE Frame 6B?

The GE Frame 6B is an industrial heavy duty gas turbine by GE Vernova. Output: 45 MW (simple cycle, ISO, natural gas). Typical applications: refineries, LNG, CHP, industrial power, island mode and remote sites. Source: GE Vernova, 6B product page.

Is the Frame 6B the same as the MS6001B?

Yes. Frame 6B and MS6001B name the same GE heavy duty gas turbine. Upgraded versions are often marked 6B.03.

What fuels can a Frame 6B burn?

The Frame 6B is known for fuel flexibility. The fuel a specific unit can burn depends on its fuel system and combustors, which are listed in the unit documents.

What is the output of a GE Frame 6B?

GE Vernova states 45 MW in simple cycle at ISO conditions on natural gas for the 6B. Used units are often rated lower depending on version and condition; the units listed here are rated 38.6 MW and 39.2 MW by their owners.
