Broker for used gas turbines and generator sets

What a broker for used turbines and generator sets does, and how to check one.

Short answer: A broker for used gas turbines and generator sets brings owners of surplus equipment and buyers together, presents the technical data, screens buyers by letter of intent, coordinates inspection and lets buyer and owner sign the purchase contract directly. LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC is an independent broker that lists gas turbines, steam turbines and generator sets on behalf of their owners.

What does a turbine broker do?

Collects and checks the unit data from the owner, gives each unit a stock number, sends offers to matching buyers, receives the letter of intent, arranges full data and inspection, and supports buyer and owner until the purchase contract is signed.

Does the broker own the equipment?

Not at LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC. The equipment belongs to its owner until the sale, and the broker does not take title to it.

How do I check a broker before I send an LOI?

Ask for the company registration, a written fee agreement, a clear statement of who signs the purchase contract and how the purchase price is paid. A reliable broker confirms availability with the owner before every offer and never asks the buyer to pay the purchase price to the broker.

What are warning signs in the used turbine market?

Requests for advance fees or deposits to the broker, long chains of intermediaries who have never seen the unit, the same unit offered at many different prices, refusal to allow an inspection, and payment instructions that change during the deal.

How is LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC paid?

By commission agreed in writing before the deal is closed. Purchase payments go from buyer to owner, or through an escrow agent or letter of credit agreed between them, and never pass through accounts of LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC. More: how we work
