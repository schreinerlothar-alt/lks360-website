Model guide

What the GE Frame 5 (MS5001) is used for

The GE Frame 5 (MS5001) is a heavy duty gas turbine with one of the longest service histories in the industry. Many units still run in power plants, refineries and on power barges.

  • Simple cycle power generation
  • Cogeneration in refineries and industry
  • Power barges and mobile power plants

GE Frame 5 (MS5001) compared with neighbouring models

The MS5001 is the single shaft version for generator drive; the MS5002 is the two shaft version for mechanical drive. The Frame 6B is the next larger GE heavy duty model.

Price

Used GE Frame 5 price: what sets it

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC does not publish prices; each GE Frame 5 is priced by its owner and quoted on request with the stock number. The price depends on these points:

  • Model: MS5001 single shaft for generator drive or MS5002 two shaft for mechanical drive, and the letter of the version.
  • Hours, starts and the last overhaul: Equivalent operating hours, starts and the date and scope of the last hot gas path or major inspection matter more than the year of manufacture.
  • Overhaul records: A unit overhauled by the manufacturer or an authorised shop, with reports, sells faster than one without documents.
  • Condition: New and unused, overhauled, in operation or preserved: each condition is priced differently.
  • Scope of supply: Generator, controls, enclosure, fuel system, inlet and exhaust system, transformer and spare parts included or not.
  • Frequency and voltage: A 60 Hz unit and a 50 Hz unit serve different markets; the generator voltage has to fit the buyer's grid.
  • Emission system: DLE, water injection or diffusion combustion decides whether the unit meets the emission limits at the buyer's site.
  • Location and logistics: Dismantling, transport of heavy lifts and import duties are part of the buyer's total cost.

The GE Frame 5 (MS5001) is an industrial heavy duty gas turbine by GE (now GE Vernova). Output: 13.3 MW (early MS5001D) to 21 MW (MS5001N) in cited installations. Typical applications: power generation in simple cycle, cogeneration and combined cycle; peaking, black start; mechanical drive. Source: Combined Cycle Journal, Special report: The venerable Frame 5 gas turbine.

What is the difference between the MS5001 and the MS5002?

The MS5001 is a single shaft gas turbine used for generator drive. The MS5002 is a two shaft design used mainly for compressor and pump drive.

Are Frame 5 gas turbines still supported?

Yes. GE and independent service providers still supply parts and service for the Frame 5 fleet.

How many Frame 5 gas turbines were built?

According to Combined Cycle Journal, well over 3,000 Frame 5 units were built in single and two shaft versions, and the single shaft MS5001 makes up about 85 percent of them.
