Model guide

What the Siemens SGT-800 is used for

The SGT-800 is a Siemens Energy industrial gas turbine at the top of the industrial output range. It goes back to the GTX100 and is often installed in combined cycle and cogeneration plants.

  • Combined cycle power plants
  • Industrial cogeneration and district heating
  • Simple cycle power for industry

Siemens SGT-800 compared with neighbouring models

The SGT-800 is the largest industrial gas turbine in the Siemens Energy range; above it start the heavy duty utility gas turbines such as the SGT5-2000E. Several ratings exist, so the rating of a used unit matters for the comparison.

Price

Siemens SGT-800 price: what sets it

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC does not publish prices; each Siemens SGT-800 is priced by its owner and quoted on request with the stock number. The price depends on these points:

  • Rating: The SGT-800 has been sold in several ratings; the rating of the unit decides the output and the comparison.
  • Hours and cycles since the last overhaul: Engine hours, starts and the date and scope of the last overhaul matter more than the year of manufacture.
  • Overhaul records: An engine back from the manufacturer or an authorised shop with full records sells faster than one without documents.
  • Condition: New and unused, zero time since overhaul, in operation or preserved: each condition is priced differently.
  • Scope of supply: Generator, enclosure, controls, fuel system, step up transformer and spare parts included or not.
  • Combined cycle scope: Whether a steam turbine, HRSG and balance of plant come with the gas turbine.
  • Frequency and voltage: A 60 Hz unit for the Americas and a 50 Hz unit for Europe, Africa or Asia are separate markets; dual frequency units widen the buyer group.
  • Emission system: DLE or water injected combustion decides whether a unit meets the emission limits at the buyer's site.
  • Location and logistics: Dismantling, transport, road permits and import duties are part of the buyer's total cost.
