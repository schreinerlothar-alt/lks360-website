Model guide

What the Siemens SGT-750 is used for

The SGT-750 is a newer Siemens Energy industrial gas turbine in the medium output class, designed for power generation as well as mechanical drive in oil and gas.

  • Industrial power generation and cogeneration
  • Compressor drive for pipelines and LNG
  • Oil and gas production sites

Siemens SGT-750 compared with neighbouring models

The SGT-750 sits in output between the SGT-700 and the SGT-800. It is a later design than both, so fewer used units reach the market.
