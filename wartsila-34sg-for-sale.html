Model guide

What the Wärtsilä 34SG is used for

The Wärtsilä 34SG is a medium speed lean burn gas engine built in several cylinder versions. It is used in gas engine power plants for base load and for flexible grid balancing.

  • Gas engine power plants
  • Combined heat and power for industry and cities
  • Flexible generation to balance renewables

Wärtsilä 34SG compared with neighbouring models

The 34SG is larger per unit than high speed engines such as the Jenbacher J620. The 20V34SG is the largest cylinder version. The Caterpillar G20CM34 is a comparable medium speed gas engine.

Price

Used Wärtsilä 34SG price: what sets it

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC does not publish prices; each Wärtsilä 34SG is priced by its owner and quoted on request with the stock number. The price depends on these points:

  • Cylinder version: 12V, 16V or 20V34SG decides the output per set.
  • Hours since overhaul: Running hours since the last major overhaul and the overhaul records.
  • Rating: Standby, prime or continuous rating; compare units only at the same rating.
  • Condition: New and unused, overhauled or in operation.
  • Scope of supply: Generator, radiators or cooling, controls, switchgear, enclosure or container, heat recovery.
  • Frequency, voltage and fuel: 50 or 60 Hz, generator voltage and the fuel or gas quality at the buyer's site.
  • Emissions and logistics: Emission limits at the site, transport and import duties.
