Compliance policy

Our role

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC is an independent broker for used and surplus power generation equipment. We do not buy, own or resell equipment. The equipment stays with its owner until the sale, and the purchase contract is made directly between buyer and owner.

Money flow

Purchase payments go directly from buyer to owner, or through a third-party escrow agent agreed between them, and never pass through accounts of LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC. Brokerage income is a commission under a written agreement, paid by bank transfer after the purchase contract is signed. We do not accept cash, crypto assets or payments from third parties who are not party to the agreement.

Know your counterparty

Before we send an offer with full data, we identify the buyer and the owner: company name, registration, address, authorised signatory and beneficial owner. We keep these records for the duration of the business relationship and the legal retention periods.

Red flags

We stop a transaction if a party refuses to identify itself, asks to route payments through us or through unrelated third parties, or asks to change the stated end user or destination.
