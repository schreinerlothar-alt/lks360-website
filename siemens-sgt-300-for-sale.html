Model guide

What the Siemens SGT-300 is used for

The SGT-300 is a Siemens Energy industrial gas turbine in the small output class. It goes back to the Tempest design and is used both for power generation and, in its twin shaft version, for driving compressors.

  • Industrial cogeneration and district heating
  • Power generation on oil and gas sites, on and offshore
  • Simple cycle power for remote sites

Siemens SGT-300 compared with neighbouring models

The SGT-300 sits between the SGT-100 and the SGT-400. It is chosen when the SGT-100 is too small and the site load does not justify an SGT-400.

What is the Siemens SGT-300?

The Siemens SGT-300 is an industrial gas turbine by Siemens Energy. Output: 8.0 MW(e) (single shaft, simple cycle). Typical applications: simple and combined cycle, industrial cogeneration, district heating, oil and gas on and offshore. Source: Siemens Energy, SGT-300 product page.

What was the SGT-300 called before?

The SGT-300 goes back to the Tempest gas turbine from the former Ruston and Alstom industrial range, now part of Siemens Energy.

What is the difference between the SGT-300 and the SGT-400?

Both are Siemens Energy industrial gas turbines. The SGT-400 has the higher output; the SGT-300 suits smaller loads. See the comparison page SGT-400 vs SGT-300 for the manufacturer data side by side.
