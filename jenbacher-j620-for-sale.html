Price

Used Jenbacher J620 price: what sets it

LKS 360-SCHREINER LLC does not publish prices; each Jenbacher J620 is priced by its owner and quoted on request with the stock number. The price depends on these points:

  • Engine version and gas type: Engine version and whether it runs on natural gas or special gases such as biogas or landfill gas.
  • Hours since overhaul: Running hours since the last major overhaul and the overhaul records.
  • Rating: Standby, prime or continuous rating; compare units only at the same rating.
  • Condition: New and unused, overhauled or in operation.
  • Scope of supply: Generator, radiators or cooling, controls, switchgear, enclosure or container, heat recovery.
  • Frequency, voltage and fuel: 50 or 60 Hz, generator voltage and the fuel or gas quality at the buyer's site.
  • Emissions and logistics: Emission limits at the site, transport and import duties.

Send the stock number to manager@lks360.com and we reply with the owner's price and conditions.
