What we do

Power generation equipment and site supervision

We work on commission. The equipment stays with its owner until the sale, and the purchase contract is made between buyer and owner.

Gas turbines

Industrial and aeroderivative gas turbine packages from about 3.5 MW to large heavy duty units, 50 and 60 Hz, new, unused or overhauled.

Steam turbines

Steam turbine generator sets for power plants and industrial cogeneration.

Generator sets

Gas and diesel generator sets for industrial and utility projects.

Site supervision

Welding and installation supervision for industrial plants. 2026: welding supervision on a stainless steel beverage plant for a German plant builder.

How we work

Four clear steps

1

Requirement

The buyer sends type, output, frequency and location.

2

Offer

We match units from owners and send an offer with the owner's conditions.

3

LOI and inspection

With a letter of intent the buyer receives full data and can inspect the unit.

4

Contract

Buyer and owner sign the purchase contract. Our commission is agreed in writing.
